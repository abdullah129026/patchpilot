# Acceptable Use (DRAFT)

Status: draft for the Phase 4 human copy pass.

Do not use PatchPilot to:

- Run malware, miners, or attack tooling through the sandbox.
- Attack third parties, probe other systems, or exfiltrate data.
- Submit repositories or issues for harassment, or to reproduce
  exploits against systems you do not own.
- Evade the job ceilings (attempts, wall time, per-IP and daily caps).

Jobs that violate this policy are refused or deleted, and abusive
sources are blocked. Abuse and copyright contact: to be published
before launch (Phase 4 launch checklist).
