# Terms of Service

Status: reviewed in the Phase 4 copy pass, 2026-11-07.
until that pass.

PatchPilot is a free demonstration tool. You paste a public GitHub
issue URL; PatchPilot attempts to reproduce the reported bug in a
sandbox and shows you what it found.

- You are responsible for the repository you point at. Do not submit
  issues for repositories you have no legitimate reason to examine.
- Output is machine-generated. A suggested fix diff is a suggestion,
  not a verified correction, unless its report says the fix was
  verified by a test transition inside the sandbox.
- Reports disclose that an AI agent produced them and separate what
  was verified from what was suggested.
- PatchPilot claims no ownership of any fix diff and no endorsement
  from any upstream project.
- Reports show the source repository's license and attribution. If a
  diff is applied and shared, it takes the source repository's license
  terms.
- Jobs may be refused or deleted at any time, and the service may be
  stopped without notice.
- Job workspaces are deleted after each job. Reports are kept (see the
  privacy note) and removed on request.
